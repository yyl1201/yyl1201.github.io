AioTool Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-09 · App: AioTool · Package: com.yaoyili.aitool

Yili Yao ("we", "us") operates the AioTool mobile application ("the App"). This policy explains what information we and our advertising partners process, why, and the choices you have.

AioTool is a collection of offline utilities. The tools themselves run entirely on your device — the text, numbers, dates and passwords you enter are never uploaded to us. Data collection is limited to what is required to serve and measure advertising.

1. What this app is

The App does not require an account and does not ask for any runtime permission such as storage, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. Everything you calculate, generate, convert or track is stored locally on your device.

2. Information that may be processed

  • Advertising identifier (GAID) — the device's Google Advertising ID, used to serve and personalize ads and to measure their performance. You can reset or opt out of it at any time in your device settings.
  • Device & app information — device model, OS version, screen metrics, language, app version, and crash or diagnostic logs.
  • Coarse location — approximate country or city level, derived from network information, and only when an advertising partner uses it for ad targeting and frequency capping. The App requests no precise-location permission.
  • In-app usage events — which tools you open or complete, ad impressions and clicks, and rewarded-video outcomes, in aggregate form, to improve the App and to manage how often ads are shown.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or files.

4. Consent (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

  • EEA / UK / Switzerland / California — before any personalized advertising, the App displays a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog. Ad-related data is then processed on the basis of your consent, and you can review or change your choices at any time in App → Settings → Ad personalization.
  • US "Do Not Sell / Share" — where required, the App honors the IAB TCF and GPP signals and offers the opt-out through the same consent tooling.

5. Your choices and opt-out

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android settings → Google → Ads.
  • Opt out of interest-based advertising: aboutads.info/choices.
  • Manage in-App ad personalization: App → Settings → Ad personalization.
  • Turning personalization off does not remove ads; it limits targeted delivery.

6. Data stored on your device

Favorites, recently used tools, saved countdown events, habit check-ins and unlocked items live only on your device. Erase them at any time via App → Settings → Clear local data. Uninstalling the App removes them completely.

7. Retention and deletion

Advertising and usage data are retained by each partner under its own policy. To ask us to act on a deletion or access request, contact 19994443997@163.com.

8. Security

Advertising and diagnostic traffic is transmitted over HTTPS. Because the tools run offline, sensitive inputs such as generated passwords never leave the device. No method of transmission or storage is one hundred percent secure.

9. Children

AioTool is a general-audience utilities app and is not directed at children under 13 or the minimum age in your region. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; if you believe a child has provided us data, contact us at the address below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change accordingly. The copy bundled inside the App (Settings → Privacy policy) is always the version in effect for the build you are running.
