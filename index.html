How it handles your data

Every tool performs its work locally on your device. Text you paste into the text tools, dates you enter, passwords and QR codes you generate, habits and countdowns you save — all of it stays on the phone; AioTool does not upload it anywhere.

AioTool does not ask you for an email address, a phone number, a location, your contacts, your camera, your microphone or your files.

Permissions

The app itself declares no Android permissions. The permissions that appear in the installed package — network access and the advertising identifier — come from the third-party advertising SDKs bundled with the free version, not from AioTool's own code.

AioTool is free and is supported by advertising. Ads may be served by Google AdMob, Unity Ads and TopOn, and those networks may process device identifiers for ad targeting and measurement. The full list, and the controls available to you, are set out in the Privacy Policy.

If a network listed in the Privacy Policy is not enabled in your region or in the current build, no data is sent to it.
